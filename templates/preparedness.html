Flood Safety & Inundation Protocol

BEFORE A FLOOD (Preparation Phase)
  • Know your area's flood risk level and nearest elevated relief shelter locations.
  • Elevate critical electrical panels, water heaters, and home appliances above flood base level.
  • Keep identity documents (Aadhaar, passports, insurance papers) in a sealed waterproof pouch.
  • Clear household drains and gutters of debris to prevent localized pooling.
DURING A FLOOD (Action Phase)
  • Turn off the main electrical circuit breaker and gas valve before leaving the premises.
  • Never walk or drive through moving flood water: 6 inches of rapid water can knock an adult down; 2 feet of water will float a car.
  • Move family and pets to upper floors or designated high-ground community camps.
  • Do not touch wet electrical switches or fallen power lines.
AFTER A FLOOD (Recovery Phase)
  • Return home only after emergency officials declare the area structurally safe.
  • Boil all drinking water for at least 1 minute or use certified purification tablets.
  • Watch out for snakes, scorpions, and rodents that may have taken refuge in dry parts of the building.
  • Photograph water damage before beginning cleanup for insurance and relief documentation.

Cyclone & Severe Storm Protocol

BEFORE A CYCLONE
  • Check house roofs, trim overgrown tree branches overhanging power lines or roof tiles.
  • Store at least 72 hours of non-perishable food, water, battery torches, and power banks.
  • Tape large glass windows with crossed adhesive tape to prevent flying glass splinters.
DURING A CYCLONE
  • Remain indoors in the strongest, windowless central room of the building.
  • Beware of the Eye of the Storm: If winds suddenly calm down, do NOT go outside; gale winds will resume violently from the opposite direction.
  • Disconnect electrical appliances to avoid storm power surges.
AFTER A CYCLONE
  • Beware of loose live wires hanging from broken electricity poles.
  • Clear stagnant water around living quarters to prevent mosquito-borne epidemics (Dengue/Malaria).

Earthquake Safety Protocol

DURING THE SHAKING: DROP, COVER, AND HOLD ON
  • DROP down onto your hands and knees.
  • COVER your head and neck under a sturdy table or desk.
  • HOLD ON to your shelter until the shaking stops.
  • If outdoors, move into an open area away from power lines, tall buildings, and glass facades.
  • Do NOT use elevators during or immediately after an earthquake.
AFTER THE QUAKE
  • Expect aftershocks. Be prepared to Drop, Cover, and Hold On again.
  • Check for gas leaks. If you smell gas, turn off the main valve and evacuate immediately.

Fire Safety & Evacuation

IN CASE OF STRUCTURAL FIRE
  • Crawl Low in Smoke: Smoke rises to the ceiling; clean breathable air is 1-2 feet above the floor.
  • Feel door handles with the back of your hand before opening. If hot, do not open—use an alternate route.
  • If your clothing catches fire: STOP, DROP, AND ROLL until flames are smothered.
  • Call 101 or 112 immediately once you are outside in a safe zone.

Tsunami Coastal Safety

CRITICAL WARNING SIGNS
  • Strong earthquake felt along the coast.
  • Rapid, unusual recession of ocean waters exposing the sea floor.
  • Loud roaring sound from the ocean resembling a freight train or jet aircraft.
  • Action: Immediately move at least 2 kilometers inland or 30 meters above sea level to high ground or multi-story concrete structures.

Landslide & Mudflow Safety

WARNING SIGNS & ACTION
  • Springs or water seepage breaking through ground in new locations.
  • Cracks appearing in building foundations, retaining walls, or pavements.
  • Faint rumbling sounds that increase in volume as landslide approaches.
  • Evacuate steep slope settlements immediately during persistent torrential rains.
