Quick First-Aid Protocols

Apply direct firm pressure to the wound with a clean cloth or bandage. Keep the injured limb elevated above heart level if possible. Do not remove embedded objects.

Place hands in the center of the chest. Push hard and fast at a rate of 100-120 beats per minute (to the rhythm of 'Stayin Alive'). Allow full chest recoil.

Cool the burn under cold running water for at least 10-15 minutes. Cover loosely with sterile plastic wrap or clean dressing. Never apply ice, butter, or oil.

Do NOT touch the victim while they are in contact with electrical source. Turn off the main circuit breaker. Use a dry wooden stick to separate victim from wire.
